# Protocol SYN-101: A Synthetic Phase II Study

This entirely fictional study evaluates investigational product SYN-A in adults with synthetic condition X. It is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study enrolling approximately 120 participants across 15 fictional sites.

## Objectives and endpoints

The primary objective is to evaluate efficacy at Week 12. The primary endpoint is change from baseline in the Synthetic Symptom Score at Week 12. Secondary endpoints include responder rate, change in quality-of-life score, and treatment-emergent adverse events.

## Visits

Screening occurs from Day -28 to Day -1. Visits occur at Baseline, Weeks 2, 4, 8, 12, and a Week 16 safety follow-up. Demographics, medical history, medications, vital signs, laboratory results, efficacy assessments, adverse events, and exposure are collected as appropriate.

## Population

Participants must be 18 through 75 years old, provide informed consent, have confirmed synthetic condition X for at least six months, and meet the minimum symptom-score threshold. Key exclusions include unstable medical disease, prohibited concomitant therapy, pregnancy, and participation in another interventional study within 30 days.

Safety is monitored through adverse events, laboratory testing, vital signs, physical examination, and an independent fictional monitoring committee.
