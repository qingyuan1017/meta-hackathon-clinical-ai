# Synthetic Statistical Analysis Plan — SYN-101

The primary analysis population is the Intent-to-Treat population, defined as all randomized participants. The Safety population includes all randomized participants who receive at least one dose. The primary endpoint is change from baseline in Synthetic Symptom Score at Week 12.

The primary analysis uses an ANCOVA model with treatment as a fixed effect and baseline score as a covariate. Least-squares means, treatment differences, two-sided 95% confidence intervals, and p-values will be presented. Missing Week 12 values will be addressed using multiple imputation in a sensitivity analysis.

Safety summaries include treatment-emergent adverse events by system organ class and preferred term, serious adverse events, laboratory shift tables, vital-sign summaries, and participant listings. Outputs are grouped by randomized treatment for efficacy and actual treatment for safety.
