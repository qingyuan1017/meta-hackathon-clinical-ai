# SDTM cSDRG narrative revision

Revise the supplied draft Study Data Reviewer's Guide only as requested by the human reviewer. Return one complete `CSDRGDocument`, preserving its study name, section order, table shapes, and all factual evidence.

Sections marked `evidence_controlled: true` are immutable. Copy their headings, bodies, todos, tables, and evidence flags exactly. Do not add execution, validation, conformance, submission-readiness, or regulatory claims that are absent from the supplied evidence. In editable sections, improve reviewer-facing narrative, organization, and explicit TODOs without inventing study facts. Keep the document labeled as a draft.
